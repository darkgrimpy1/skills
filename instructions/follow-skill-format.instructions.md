When a skill or instruction prescribes a format, structure, or template, follow it EXACTLY. Do NOT infer the format from surrounding codebase style, existing files, or your own judgement. The skill is the source of truth — existing files may predate it or violate it. If a skill defines a skeleton, headings, columns, or rules, reproduce them verbatim; resolve any conflict in favour of the skill, not the codebase.
